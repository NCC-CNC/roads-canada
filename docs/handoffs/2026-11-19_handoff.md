# Session Handoff: 2026-11-19 (fourth session)

## Summary

Worked issue #4 (provincial datasets) across two PRs: #25 (merged) and this
one. The National Road Network (NRN) and provincial datasets with resource
roads went through the same pipeline as OSM, with a documented vehicle-road
filter each. Main finding: OSM and the NRN overstate roadless area wherever a
dataset with resource and low-use roads exists, by 9 to 36 points. Notes 10
and 11 hold the tables and findings.
