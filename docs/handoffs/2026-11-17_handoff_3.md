## Summary

Built the first scripted OSM roadless-area prototype (issue #3) and validated
it on Nova Scotia against Table 4 of Poley et al. (2022). With a 2021 OSM
snapshot, roadless area is within about 1 to 2% of the paper. Patch count,
mean and median do not match because of the land mask. Details in
`docs/design/05_osm_prototype_nova_scotia.md`.

## Completed

- Settled with the user: test region Nova Scotia; OSM filter is the paper's
  exclusion list (bridleway, cycleway, footway, path, pedestrian, steps).
- `src/roadless/osm_roadless.py`: filter, 1 km buffer, difference from land,
  patch metrics, GeoParquet output (about one minute for Nova Scotia).
- `src/roadless/fetch_inputs.py`: Geofabrik snapshot by date and province
  boundary from geoBoundaries (StatCan-derived, pinned commit).
- `pyproject.toml` and `uv.lock` (uv, GeoPandas, pyogrio, Shapely, DuckDB,
  pyarrow). `CLAUDE.md` Commands and `CONTRIBUTING.md` Setup filled in.
- Corrected the earlier claim that the 2021 OSM extract is gone: Geofabrik
  keeps public 1 January snapshots back to 2014 (only April 2021 is missing).

## Current state

| OSM snapshot | Roadless km2 | Percent | Patches |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2021-01-01 | 11,728 | 20.5 | 3,816 |

- Roadless area and percent roadless are the pass/fail metrics. Patch count,
  mean and median depend on coastal slivers (81% of patches are under 1 km2
  and hold 3% of area; most touch the coast), so they are secondary.
- No tests or lint yet. Only Nova Scotia has been run. The StatCan boundary
  file returned 403 to automated download; do not work around that.
- The seismic date tally and the three damaged Table 4 cells are still open.
- Data in `data/raw/` is git-ignored and lives on the OneDrive mount.

## Risks or decisions needed

- The paper's land mask is unknown, so exact patch metrics may not be
  reproducible.
- Large provinces may expose the vector buffer memory limit flagged in ADR
  0002; the raster fallback needs a new ADR if so.
- Licence risks from the earlier handoffs stand.
- Working preference: do not fan out to parallel subagents or workflows unless
  the user asks.
