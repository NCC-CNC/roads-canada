## Current state

- `main` holds docs only. CI is the template's stack-neutral `repo-hygiene` job.
- The compilation is at `data/external/poley2022/canada_linear_features/`.
  Use `roads.gdb` (layer `roads_merge`, 7,138,352 features) or the 13
  per-province shapefiles. Do not use `all_roads_canada.shp`: it is truncated at
  the 2 GiB shapefile limit and holds only about 29% of the features. The merged
  seismic shapefile hits the same limit and failed its CRC check on extraction;
  the per-jurisdiction seismic files are fine.
- The merged roads total about 3.18 million km. OSM supplies only about 3% of
  it; most is government data. About half of the length is `track`.
- `GEN_CLASS` needs normalizing before use: mixed casing, the typos
  `unclassifed` and `seconday`, and inconsistent `_link` spellings.
- No construction or closure dates exist in the roads. Date-bearing fields exist
  only in the seismic layers (AB and YK `YEAR`, NT `Start_Year` and `End_Year`,
  BC plan and permit dates). Their coverage has not been tallied.
- The Supporting Information gives the 2021 OSM filter: drop `fclass` bridleway,
  cycleway, footway, path, pedestrian and steps, keep everything else. Roadless
  means at least 1 km from a road. The 2021 OSM extract cannot be re-downloaded.
- The authors' roadless-area layers and code are not published.
- The `Commands` section of `CLAUDE.md` and `Setup` in `CONTRIBUTING.md` are
  pending until the pipeline prototype exists.
- The data survey is web research with many items marked unverified; several
  portals blocked automated fetching (Yukon, parts of BC and QC).
- `geopandas` is not installed yet; `ogrinfo` is available for ad hoc queries.
  Queries over the geodatabase on the OneDrive mount are slow (minutes).

## Risks or decisions needed

- The compilation's metadata says it is "available via private share only", with
  a placeholder citation URL and no named licence. Do not redistribute it or
  derived products until the co-authors and WCS Canada settle this.
- Licence choice for code and data (GPL-3.0 inherited from the template; NCC may
  prefer MIT or Apache-2.0 for code and CC-BY for data). Needs an owner.
- The compilation was merged by hand with visual cleanup, so a scripted update
  cannot reproduce it exactly. See `03_poley_baseline_dataset.md`.
- Vector buffering at national scale may be too slow or memory-heavy. ADR 0002
  names a raster fallback; revisit after #3.
- The optional solo-maintainer auto-merge workflows are not enabled (they need a
  personal access token secret).
- Working preference: do not fan out to parallel subagents or workflows unless
  the user asks.
