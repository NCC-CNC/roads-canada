## Current state

- Findings from note 04 that matter most:
  - The merge includes provincial layers the paper never compared (MB MLI 20K,
    SK Human Footprint, NB Forest Roads, NL Resource Roads, PEI and Yukon
    forest roads). The merged layer is 2.12 times the paper's best dataset in
    NB, 1.74 in MB, 1.35 in SK and 1.26 in NL.
  - OSM is under 7% of merged length in ten of 13 jurisdictions. It is large
    only in NL (29%), YT (24%), NU (43%) and NT (11%).
  - NRN matches the paper's totals within 0.2% in AB, SK, NB, NS, NL and QC,
    but not in NT (5,317 km against 7,401 km) or NU (1,261 km against 912 km).
  - BEAD (Boreal Ecosystem Anthropogenic Disturbance, hand-digitized from
    30 m Landsat) supplies about 16,000 km in MB and NT. Treat it as
    lower-precision, single-date data.
- Table 4 text extraction dropped digits in three cells (AB OSM and ABHFP
  roadless area, ON GRIP roadless area). The AB OSM figure above is inferred
  from the 57%, not read from the page.
- The seismic date fields are still untallied.
- Tally scripts and raw output were in the session scratchpad and are not
  kept. They are one `ogrinfo -dialect SQLite` query per province (group by
  `SOURCE_DS`, and by `SOURCE_DS` and lower-cased `GEN_CLASS`); each province
  took several minutes on the OneDrive mount.
