## Completed

- `osm_change.py`: roadless change between two snapshots, plus an incremental
  shortcut (buffer only new `osm_id`s). Tested on NS, NB, NL (2021 vs 2022).
  Roadless change -104, -533, -284 km2. Share of new road length inside old
  roadless land varies a lot by region (5%, 22%, 35.5%).
- `run_regions.py`: resumable runner (zip check, done marker, errors.log,
  continues on failure). Baseline for all 13 jurisdictions on the 2021-01-01
  snapshot, no errors, about 8 minutes of compute in total.
- `osm_diff_probe.py`: Geofabrik per-region daily diffs for New Brunswick (30
  days). With a base extract via pyosmium, 1,455 of 1,456 changed road ways
  resolve. Edits are mostly not remote (2.3% of edited length), new ways are
  where remote change shows. `fetch_inputs.py pbf` fetches the base extract.
- Rejected: coarser buffer arcs (`quad_segs`), slower and biased.
- Corrected an earlier wrong explanation of the Nova Scotia incremental gap
  (note 08).
- New dependency: `osmium` (pyosmium 4.3).
