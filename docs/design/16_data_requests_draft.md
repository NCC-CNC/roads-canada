# Draft data requests: SK, PEI and MB forestry

Status: drafts for the project owner to review and send. Nothing has been sent.
These cover the jurisdictions where we still have only OSM and the NRN (see
note 15), which are ceilings on roadless area.

## Saskatchewan (contact `egis@gov.sk.ca`)

Subject: Request for a provincial road or forest access road layer

Hello,

We are updating Canada-wide roadless-area estimates (a 1 km buffer around
vehicle roads), following Poley et al. 2022. For Saskatchewan we only have
OpenStreetMap and the National Road Network, which miss many forestry and
resource roads. Does the province publish, or can it share, a layer of
provincial roads that includes forestry, resource and other low-use roads?
Information on its licence, its edition date and whether it carries a road
class or status field would help us choose the right filter.

We would credit the source in any result we publish.

Thank you.

## Prince Edward Island (provincial forestry or environment branch)

Subject: Request for the provincial forest roads layer

Hello,

We are updating Canada-wide roadless-area estimates, following Poley et al.
2022. For Prince Edward Island we have OpenStreetMap and the National Road
Network. A forest roads layer was previously shared for research. Is there a
current version we may use, and under what licence? Edition date and any
road status field would be helpful.

We would credit the source in any result we publish.

Thank you.
